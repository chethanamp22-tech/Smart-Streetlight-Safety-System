# Smart-Streetlight-Safety-System
Arduino-based streetlight safety system project for public safety and automation
Aim:To develop an automated street light safety system that improves public safety and road visibility while saving electrical energy using smart sensors and Arduino.
Components:Arduino Uno R3,LDR sensor,pir sensor,sound sensor,active buzzer,breadboard,jumpercircuits,led,button.
Working:
The Smart Streetlight Safety System uses multiple sensors to improve safety and automation. The LDR (Light Dependent Resistor) detects the surrounding light intensity and automatically turns the streetlights ON during low light or nighttime conditions. 

The PIR sensor detects human or vehicle movement near the streetlight area. When motion is detected, the system activates the lighting for better visibility and safety. 

A sound sensor is used to detect loud sounds such as shouting, alarms, or emergency noise. When abnormal sound is detected, the buzzer gets activated to provide an alert indication. 

The Arduino processes all sensor inputs and controls the streetlight system automatically based on environmental conditions and safety requirements.

Future Improvements
- Integration of a GSM module for sending emergency alert messages.
- Addition of an emergency push button for quick safety assistance.
